## Context

Running a smart grid means seeing and controlling the distribution network in
much finer detail than legacy systems allowed. Ørsted needed a new Distribution
Management System, and the procurement had to define what that system must do
before any vendor could build it.

## What I did

I developed the technical tender specification for the new Distribution
Management System, setting out the architecture and the capabilities needed to
enable smart-grid operations. This drew on the integration and event-driven work
across the energy systems and translated the operational goals into requirements
a supplier could deliver against.

## Outcome

Ørsted had a clear, technically grounded specification to procure a Distribution
Management System fit for smart-grid operations, reducing the risk in a large and
long-lived investment.
