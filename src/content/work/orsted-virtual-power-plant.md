## Context

At Ørsted, then DONG Energy, the shift toward renewables meant coordinating many
distributed energy assets rather than a few large plants. That needed integration
across systems that were never designed to talk to each other in real time.

## What I did

I designed service-oriented and event-driven integrations and delivered PowerHub,
a Virtual Power Plant that brings distributed assets together so they can be
operated as one. The same integration work produced SMS gateways and monitoring
that gave operations visibility across the estate. I worked as product owner,
developer, and architect across these systems, setting the technical direction
as the energy business modernised.

## Outcome

Ørsted gained a working Virtual Power Plant and the integration backbone to run
distributed energy as a coordinated whole, an early step in the company's move
toward renewable operations.
