## Context

Scrive runs e-signature services where trust and uptime are the product. The
operation ran on AWS and needed lower cost, and GDPR meant some customers needed
their data hosted inside the EU.

## What I did

I cut yearly operational cost by around 10% by optimising the AWS setup and the
database IOPS, rather than by re-platforming the service. Separately, to meet
GDPR, I set up an EU-hosted version of the application on Kubernetes, so customers
who needed their data kept inside the EU had it. Building that version on
containers and Kubernetes introduced that way of working to Scrive, and it was
recognised internally for enabling the company to adopt containers.

## Outcome

Scrive ran around 10% cheaper a year, with an EU-hosted option for customers who
needed it under GDPR and a container way of working that the wider organisation
could build on.
