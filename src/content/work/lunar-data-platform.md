## Context

Lunar is a Nordic challenger bank where data is spread across the systems that run
the business. The bank wanted that data brought together onto one platform that
data engineers and the business could build on, rather than reaching into each
system on its own.

## What I did

I led a new data platform project on Google Cloud and took it through strategic
planning, setting the outcome it needed to reach, the shape of the platform, and
the path to consolidate the bank's data onto it. It sat inside the developer and
platform experience department, so it was planned as a self-service capability for
data engineers rather than a one-off pipeline, under the same outcome targets and
continuous regulatory compliance as the rest of the department. I built the
investor pitch deck centred on this data platform, putting its value proposition
and growth potential into business terms.

## Outcome

Lunar had a defined data platform project on Google Cloud, a plan to bring its
data onto one consolidated and self-service footing, and an investor story built
around it.
