## Context

AXON's orchestration platform turns high-volume network telemetry into
operational insight. It processes up to 50,000 datapoints per second per device,
so every decision about data flow and model placement has to respect tight
latency budgets. I lead the engineering and AI organisation for this in EMEA,
around 40 people, and support integration with another 70 across Europe.

## What I did

I defined the target cloud architecture for the platform and built a new AI/ML
team focused on LLM-driven agents and applied intelligence. We scaled the
real-time data pipelines so AI and LLM models operate directly on
high-frequency, low-latency streams rather than on stale snapshots. Alongside
that work I led the move from Java to Go for the services that needed
predictable performance, integrated a design and UX workflow, and started a
third-party device team.

I also led the integration of the ACS system into the platform and supported
platform integration after acquisitions. The common thread was making good
outcomes repeatable: continuous delivery, clear ownership, and a culture where
teams can ship without waiting on a central bottleneck.

## Outcome

The platform now runs real-time pipelines that feed operational intelligence and
agent-based workflows on live telemetry. The AI/ML team is established and
shipping, the architecture holds the latency the product needs, and the
organisation has moved from a modern engineering setup toward AI-driven
operations.
