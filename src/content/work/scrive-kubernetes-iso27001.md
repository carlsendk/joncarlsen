## Context

Scrive runs e-signature services where trust and uptime are the product. The
operation needed lower cost and better service levels, and the business needed
the certifications that enterprise customers ask for.

## What I did

I led the move to containers and Kubernetes with zero downtime, which improved
the service SLA and reduced operational cost. I prepared the organisation for and
passed audits for ISO 27001 and ISAE 3000, and strengthened the incident-response
process through a cross-functional improvement team. Alongside the technical work
I converted team leads into managers through coaching and mentorship.

## Outcome

Scrive ran on a more reliable and cheaper platform, held the certifications its
customers required, and had a stronger incident process and a more capable
management layer to keep it that way.
