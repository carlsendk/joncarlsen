# ADR-002: Single public leadership identity, with tailoring kept offline

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-06-09

## Context

The owner's `about-me.md` deliberately casts wide across four target tracks:
leadership (VP/Director/CTO/CTPO), consulting (fractional/interim/advisory),
product (technical/platform PM), and program/transformation management. That
breadth is correct for a job hunt, because every application is tailored from a
master source.

The public site is the opposite situation: it is one fixed URL pasted into
applications and LinkedIn, read by a recruiter in 7-8 seconds. A page that
presents four identities at once dilutes the scan and reads as undecided. The
owner confirmed the public site should present a single, sharp identity.

## Decision

The public site presents one identity: a senior engineering leader
(Director/VP/CTO level) whose edge is GenAI and LLM-driven intelligence on
real-time data, plus platform engineering and organisational scaling. The hero,
summary, highlights, and case studies all reinforce this one story.

Per-role tailoring across the consulting, product, and program tracks stays
offline, in the existing `docs/Content/` job-folder workflow (master CV ->
tailored per-application CV and cover letter). The site does not try to serve
all four tracks; it is the leadership anchor that those tailored documents link
back to.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Explicit multi-track site

- **Description**: Surface all four tracks (leadership, consulting, product,
  program) as distinct angles on the page.
- **Pros**: Maximum flexibility; signals openness to several role types.
- **Cons**: Dilutes the 7-8s scan; reads as undecided; weakens the single
  shareable link.
- **Why rejected**: A public link should make one strong claim; breadth is
  better expressed in tailored per-job documents.

### Alternative 2: Leadership identity with a dual-track hint

- **Description**: Lead with leadership but add a short line signalling openness
  to fractional/advisory work.
- **Pros**: Slightly widens appeal with low dilution.
- **Cons**: Still introduces a second message into the hero scan.
- **Why rejected**: Owner chose the cleanest single-identity option; an advisory
  signal can be added later without redesign if wanted.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Frontpage and `/cv` carry one coherent, decisive message that lands fast.
- Content decisions (which highlights, which case studies, which expertise to
  lead with) have a clear filter: does it strengthen the leadership identity.

### Negative

- Consulting/product/program-specific framing is absent from the public site;
  candidates for those tracks rely on the tailored offline documents.

### Risks

- A role on another track might undervalue the leadership-led page. Mitigation:
  the tailored per-job CV/cover letter, not the site, carries track-specific
  positioning; the site remains the credible anchor.

## Implementation Notes

Copy and section ordering should consistently lead with leadership scale,
real-time data / AI, and platform engineering. Defer wording specifics to
content drafting in the task phase.
