## Context

eSignatur is a small Danish e-signature company where trust and uptime are the
product. As CTO I owned the full technology remit and needed to set a clear
direction, get the delivery practices in shape, and chart a credible path to the
cloud.

## What I did

I led the technology organisation, holding product ownership of the e-signature
product and owning developers, QA, and support, along with security, compliance,
the product and technical roadmap, and the back-office applications. I assessed
the current state and set a product and technical roadmap aligned with the
business strategy, and devised a cloud-adoption plan using a cloud-native maturity
matrix. I drove eSignatur to ISAE 3000 certification, the assurance report its
customers rely on. To get delivery in shape I set up the development organisation
and its engineering practices, using Azure DevOps for an optimal process flow. I
managed and onboarded sourcing partners, covering vendor selection, contracts, and
SLAs, led the move to Agile to improve collaboration and productivity, and coached
and mentored the team while closing skill gaps with training. I also brought
technical and product expertise into pre-sale meetings and ran regular customer
follow-up meetings.

## Outcome

eSignatur had a technology organisation with a clear roadmap, a costed plan to
move to the cloud, an ISAE 3000 assurance report behind it, and stronger delivery
practices. The company was later acquired by Scrive, where I kept ownership of
eSignatur and took on the wider service operations.
