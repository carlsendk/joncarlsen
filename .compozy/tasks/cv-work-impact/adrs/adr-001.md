# ADR-001: "Work" + "Impact" naming with the bullet as the connective unit

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-06-10

## Context

The site presents the owner's experience across a frontpage, a full `/cv`, a
`/projects` index, and per-project case studies at `/work/<slug>`. Three things
are currently disconnected: the company role blocks (description + achievement
bullets), the project case studies (which carry `metrics`), and the impact
highlights (`cvData.impact`, authored separately). A reader cannot move from an
impact number to the project that produced it, and "projects" undersells
leadership achievements (org-building, transformations) that are not build work.

The owner reframed the model during brainstorming: impact is attached to
projects, and the impact *is* the achievement. There is no separate
"achievements" entity floating without a project behind it. He also wants the
company surface to carry a description plus bullets that link to the work, and
the home page to let a reader click an impact number or bullet to learn more.

The site is static with near-zero JS, so the model must resolve to pre-built
pages and links, not runtime querying.

## Decision

Adopt **"Work"** as the umbrella for projects and **"Impact"** as a dedicated,
auto-aggregated achievements view. Make the **company role bullet the single
connective unit**. Each bullet does three jobs at once:

1. States the achievement (e.g. "Cut deploy time from 2 weeks to 30 minutes").
2. Carries the impact number as part of the bullet.
3. Deep-links to the work case study that tells the full story.

From this one spine:

- The **company role block** (description + linked bullets) is the company
  surface. It is authored once and reused on the home page, `/cv`, and `/impact`.
- The **`/impact` page** is a cross-cut of every metric-bearing bullet, pulled
  across all companies, each clickable to its work case study.
- The **home page** renders impact numbers and bullets as clickable entry points
  to the same work destination.
- A dedicated **`/company/<slug>` page** is an optional later enhancement that
  reuses the same bullets; it is not required to deliver the core loop.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Keep "Projects" as the umbrella

- **Description**: Fold achievements into the existing "Projects" vocabulary and
  `/projects` route.
- **Pros**: No naming change; least churn.
- **Cons**: "Projects" skews individual-contributor / build-oriented and
  undersells leadership achievements; keeps impact and projects disconnected.
- **Why rejected**: Does not solve the owner's stated tension that "projects" is
  the wrong word for the combined list.

### Alternative 2: Separate "achievement" content type alongside projects

- **Description**: Model achievements as their own entries distinct from
  projects.
- **Pros**: Allows achievements with no backing project.
- **Cons**: Contradicts the owner's framing (impact = the project's
  achievement); creates a second thing to author and keep in sync.
- **Why rejected**: The owner confirmed every achievement traces to a project,
  so a separate type adds duplication without value.

### Alternative 3: Company-first hub (dedicated company pages required, built first)

- **Description**: Build `/company/<slug>` pages as the central hub before the
  Impact page.
- **Pros**: Strongest company narrative immediately.
- **Cons**: Heavier upfront modeling; delays the high-value Impact loop; risks
  duplicate content on a single-person site.
- **Why rejected**: The connective-bullet model makes a heavy company page
  optional; the core value is the impact-to-project loop, which should ship
  first.

## Consequences

### Positive

- One authored source (the bullet) powers home, `/cv`, and `/impact` — no
  duplicate content to drift out of sync.
- A reader can move from any impact number to the project that produced it.
- Leadership achievements read at the right altitude under "Work" + "Impact"
  rather than "Projects".

### Negative

- Bullets gain structure (text + optional metric + optional work link), a small
  authoring overhead versus a plain string.
- "Impact" and "achievement" become synonyms in the product; copy and labels
  must use them consistently to avoid confusing readers.

### Risks

- Over-linking every bullet could make `/cv` noisy. Mitigation: only bullets
  with a backing case study link out; metric-only bullets need no link.
- Readers may expect a literal "Projects" nav item. Mitigation: keep a redirect
  or alias from `/projects` to `/work` during transition.

## Implementation Notes

- The connective unit lives on the company role (`cvData.roles[].bullets`),
  which becomes a structured object (text, optional metric, optional work slug)
  rather than a plain string.
- `/projects` is renamed/aliased to `/work`; `/impact` is a new derived page.
- Static build only: all links and aggregation are resolved at build time.
