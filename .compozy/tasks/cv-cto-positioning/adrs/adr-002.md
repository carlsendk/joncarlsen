# ADR-002: Truth-first enrichment with a supply-list and source-side placeholders

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-06-10

## Context

A strong CTO CV shows P&L and budget ownership, cost savings, SLA before/after,
and board cadence. The owner's own material flags these as missing ("add
figures"). The project rule is that content derives from real material and no
metric is invented. At the same time, the owner wants the highest-value missing
information identified so he can supply it, without blocking the re-pitch.

## Decision

Enrich the CV from real content first, then make the gaps explicit and fillable:

- Add only facts and figures present in the owner's source files. Recast existing
  work into business-outcome and executive-scope language without inventing
  numbers.
- Produce a single "information to supply" checklist of the high-value missing
  items (for example budget owned, cloud-cost reduction, SLA before/after,
  board/investor cadence, hiring velocity, retention), each tied to where it would
  strengthen the CV.
- Use placeholders only in the editable source or the checklist, clearly marked,
  so the owner can drop a real figure in later. Placeholders never appear as
  published text on the live pages.
- Never publish invented figures, and never bring contact details onto the site
  (the source docs contain email and phone; these are excluded, per cv-content
  ADR-002).

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Owner supplies all numbers now

- **Pros**: Hard financial impact on the CV immediately.
- **Cons**: Blocks the re-pitch on data the owner may not have to hand.
- **Why rejected**: The owner chose to enrich from existing content first and
  receive a supply-list.

### Alternative 2: Qualitative signals only

- **Pros**: Simplest; no chasing numbers.
- **Cons**: Leaves the known high-value financial gaps invisible, so they never
  get filled.
- **Why rejected**: The owner wants the gaps surfaced as an actionable list.

## Consequences

### Positive

- The live CV stays truthful and free of placeholder text.
- The owner gets a concrete, prioritised list of what to provide to reach full
  CTO-grade impact.

### Negative

- The CV will have real but not-yet-quantified claims until the owner supplies
  figures.

### Risks

- Placeholders leak to the published site. Mitigation: placeholders live only in
  the editable source or checklist and are gated out of published pages; the
  existing no-placeholder content gate guards against leaks.
