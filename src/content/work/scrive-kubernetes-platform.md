## Context

Scrive runs e-signature services where trust and uptime are the product. The
operation ran on EC2 and needed lower cost and better service levels, and
enterprise customers increasingly wanted their data kept inside the EU.

## What I did

I led the move from EC2 to containers and Kubernetes with zero downtime, which
improved the service SLA without interrupting the customers using the service. I
cut operational cost by around 10% by optimising the AWS setup and the database
IOPS, and I stood up an EU-hosted version of the application on Kubernetes so
customers who needed it could keep their data inside the EU. The move was
recognised internally for enabling Scrive to adopt containers as a way of working.

## Outcome

Scrive ran on a more reliable and roughly 10% cheaper platform, with an
EU-hosted option for customers who required it and a container way of working
that the wider organisation could build on.
