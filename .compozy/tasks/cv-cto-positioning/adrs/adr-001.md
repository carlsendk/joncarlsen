# ADR-001: Re-pitch the canonical CV to CTO altitude in one pass

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-06-10

## Context

The owner is targeting CTO and senior engineering-leadership roles. He has strong
raw material for this (a CTO-framed CV PDF, a documented CTO/CTPO positioning
angle, investor-pitch and advisory-board signals, a published leadership
framework, a business degree), but the live site reads more like a Director of
Engineering: technical activity rather than executive scope and business outcome.
Research on CTO CVs confirms the gap is altitude of language and scope of
ownership, plus signals a head-of-engineering CV does not carry (board/investor
interface, strategy, technology vision, business-outcome framing). The structured
tagged content source and the public site (frontpage, /cv, /projects) already
exist from the cv-content and cv-content-improve features.

## Decision

Treat this as a single content feature that re-pitches the existing canonical
public CV to CTO altitude, enriched from the owner's source documents. In one
pass:

- Recast the CV spine: an executive summary and a hard scope line, impact recast
  as business outcomes, role bullets rewritten in ownership and outcome language.
- Add a leadership-approach / technology-vision section.
- Add a prominent CTO credentials grouping (eSignatur CTO remit, Lunar investor
  pitch deck, Heeplink advisory board, Scalers CTO/CPO network, business degree).
- Feature the published thought-leadership writing as external-brand evidence.
- Add a few more strategic projects from the owner's project history.
- Keep one canonical CTO-ready leadership identity; no separate variant page.

This builds on the existing structure and design system rather than adding new
architecture.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative B: Phased (enrich source, then re-present)

- **Description**: Phase 1 loads CTO material into the source with little visible
  change; Phase 2 recasts the presentation.
- **Pros**: Lower risk per step.
- **Cons**: Delays the visible CTO re-pitch the owner asked for; splits one
  content pass into two.
- **Why rejected**: The owner wants the CTO-ready site delivered, not deferred.

### Alternative C: Presentation-led, lean content

- **Description**: Recast the spine and add an executive summary and credentials
  from the strongest existing facts; defer extra projects and the vision section.
- **Pros**: Fastest to a CTO-looking site.
- **Cons**: Drops the leadership-vision section and added projects the owner
  explicitly chose.
- **Why rejected**: Omits scope the owner selected.

## Consequences

### Positive

- The canonical CV reads CTO-ready: executive scope, business outcomes, vision,
  credentials, external brand.
- All enrichment draws from one structured source, so it compounds across the
  site and any later targeted variant.

### Negative

- A larger single content pass; more copy to write and review to the voice bar.

### Risks

- Over-claiming at CTO altitude without hard figures. Mitigation: ADR-002
  (truth-first enrichment, gaps tracked, nothing invented).
