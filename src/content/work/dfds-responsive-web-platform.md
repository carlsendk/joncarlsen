## Context

Before this work DFDS served customers through web applications that were not
built for mobile and were slow to change. The customer-experience teams needed a
way to release more often and to meet visitors on the device they actually used.

## What I did

I scaled the department from two teams to five and moved the customer-facing
architecture to React, serverless functions, and a headless CMS. We released
DFDS's first responsive website, added A/B and multivariate testing across the
web teams, and brought design and UX into the delivery flow. Continuous delivery
and infrastructure as code made releases routine rather than risky.

## Outcome

DFDS gained a modern, mobile-ready web platform and teams that could experiment
and ship on their own cadence. The testing and delivery practices set here became
the way the web teams worked, and the architecture carried the customer
experience for years.
